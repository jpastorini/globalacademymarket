Questions

How do I get the guide?

Right after payment you receive an email with your download links. The guide reads well on a phone; print the vein maps and recipe cards from any printer.

Is anything shipped?

No. Everything is digital (PDF). You buy the paint, glaze and brushes yourself at any hardware store; the shopping list tells you what to get.

I'm not artistic. Can I really do this?

The method is built for that. You practise three movements on boards, follow a colour recipe, and copy a printed vein map instead of inventing veins. Most people need two practice boards before the wall.

How long does a wall take?

Plan an evening for prep and the base coat, then about one day to marble an 8 × 10 ft wall slab by slab, and a few hours over the next day to seal it.

Can I do it in a bathroom?

Yes on walls outside the shower, with good ventilation and a water-based clear coat. Not inside showers, on floors or on countertops you cut on; the guide explains where it works and where it doesn't.

What if I mess up?

Almost every mistake can be glazed over: let it dry, roll a thin wash of base colour mixed with glaze, and redo that slab. The fix-it chart covers the twelve most common problems.

Are the images photos of real walls?

No. They are illustrations we made to show the colours, vein patterns and stages, so you can see what you're aiming for.

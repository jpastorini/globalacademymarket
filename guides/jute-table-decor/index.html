Freehand coils drift because nothing tells your hands where the next row goes. A full-size template with the coil path printed on it does. Glue the outline first, fill inward, hide the tail underneath, and it looks like it came from a shop.

Questions

How do I get the kit?

Right after payment you receive an email with your download links. The guide reads well on a phone; print the templates from any computer or phone connected to a printer.

Is anything shipped?

No. Everything is digital: PDF guide, PDF templates and PDF bonuses. You buy the cord, felt and glue yourself; the cheat sheet tells you what to get.

Do I need to draw or sew?

No. You print the template, place your base on it or trace it, and glue the cord along the printed lines. A hot glue gun and scissors are the main tools.

My printer only takes regular paper. Will big pieces fit?

Yes. Large pieces are split across several pages with overlap and alignment marks. You tape them together; the print guide shows how. Every page has a check square so you know it printed at true size.

Can I wash them?

Spot clean only. Jute doesn't like soaking or the washing machine. The care card explains how to clean and store them.

Can I sell what I make?

Yes, you can gift or sell the pieces you make. Please don't share or resell the template files themselves.

Questions

How do I get the kit?

Right after payment you receive an email with your download links. The guide and sheets read well on a phone; print the hole ruler and the tags from any printer at 100% size.

Is anything shipped?

No. Everything is digital (PDF). You buy the thongs, cotton yarn and a hook yourself; the guide tells you what to look for.

Which thongs or jandals do I use?

Any plain, cheap pair with a firm sole, one size smaller than you normally wear. The care card has a quick firm-or-flimsy test so you don't buy a pair that tears.

I'm a beginner. Can I do this?

If you can chain and single crochet, yes. The first family of designs is rated easy, every row ends with its stitch count, and the Ring Method takes the hardest step, the first row into the sole, out of your hands and gives it to a tapestry needle.

Does the yarn rip through the sole?

That happens when a hook is forced through rubber with every stitch. In this kit you make the holes with the smallest tool, blanket-stitch a ring with a needle, and crochet into the ring. The load is spread over the whole ring instead of one hole.

Does it cover my size?

Every sheet has seven hole-count columns, which cover roughly AU/NZ women's 5–11 (US 6–12, UK 3–9, EU 36–42). You count your own holes, so it also works for wide feet and for soles marked with odd sizes.

US or UK crochet terms?

The sheets use US terms, with the UK term in brackets the first time each stitch appears, and the cheat sheet has the full conversion table.

About

Practical guides for hands-on skills.

An independent publisher of practical guides

Global Academy Market publishes short, visual guides for hands-on skills: crafts and trades you can learn at home and, if you want, turn into a small side income.

How we make a guide

Each guide is written and reviewed by our editorial team, based on hands-on practice and on the practitioners and published sources we consult for that skill. We list the materials you actually need, put the steps in order, and point out the mistakes beginners usually make. Every guide is laid out to read comfortably on a phone.

What we promise, and what we don't

  • Clear instructions in plain language.
  • Honest expectations. We never promise income. Any numbers about selling what you make are examples, not results to expect. See our results disclaimer.
  • A 30-day money-back guarantee on every guide. See our refund policy.
